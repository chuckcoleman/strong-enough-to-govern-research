# Coding Protocol

## Comparative cases

For every included case, record:

1. case-selection frame and inclusion rationale;
2. attempted institutional control;
3. chokepoint;
4. accountability function(s) affected;
5. institutional response;
6. immediate outcome;
7. whether correction remained timely and reversible;
8. durability of the response or restoration;
9. evidence that weakens or limits the proposed mechanism.

Do not use democracy indices as causal estimators. Attribute contested labels and interpretations to their sources.

## Timing episodes

Record, where available: initiating event, subpoena/order/request date, legal filing, trial decision, appellate decision, compliance/accommodation, relevant political or administrative endpoint, and whether relief still could alter that endpoint. Distinguish legitimate procedural time from obstructive or consequence-changing delay.

## Canonical options

For each option, record primary dependencies, plausible common-mode failures, automatic/nonjudicial components, litigation or enforcement exposure, and unresolved capacity tradeoffs. Do not collapse these dimensions into a score.

## Structural cases

Separate representational-fairness claims from anti-entrenchment claims. A structural case belongs in the central mechanism only if there is a defensible chain from the rule to control, operation, or durability of an accountability institution.
