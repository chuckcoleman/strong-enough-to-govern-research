# Proposal Architecture Decisions — v0.12

**Date:** 2026-10-03

- Six provisional protected function families: election finalization; bounded emergency authority; domestic coercive/military authority; neutral administrative capacity; timely oversight; non-impunity clemency.
- Courts remain cross-cutting enforcement infrastructure rather than a seventh red line.
- Senate, House/redistricting, and electoral-design chapters remain structural reinforcements and are retained only to the extent evidence connects them to accountability performance or safeguard durability.
- The Nixon–McGahn contrast anchors the timing claim.
- Electoral-design claims remain narrower than formal voting theory: Scotland and Portland demonstrate STV implementation feasibility; the standalone cap experiment is engineering evidence, not evidence of democratic resilience.
- STV ranking-cap experiment: Scottish caps 4–8 changed winner sets in 14, 7, 1, 0, and 0 of 1,049 elections; Portland caps 4 and 5 changed no winner set in four districts but would truncate substantial observed rankings; ranks above six are source-censored in Portland.
- Durability must report cumulative threat-level resilience and individual dependency sensitivity separately.
- Civic-reinforcement material remains in the Epilogue and does not substitute for enforceable institutional safeguards.
