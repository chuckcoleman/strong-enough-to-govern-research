# Research Decisions

**Date:** 2026-10-01

- Domestic coercive/military authority remains an explicit omitted/red-line candidate rather than being assumed to be fully covered by emergency powers. Its legal pathway and accountability functions are sufficiently distinct to require direct testing.
- The comparative case set is selected by outcome variation and a common coding template, not narrative convenience.
- The timing argument is treated as an empirical mechanism question: when does delay make otherwise valid correction practically ineffective?
- Chapters on Senate, House/redistricting, and electoral design remain structural reinforcements rather than red-line families. Their claims are retained only where empirical evidence connects them to accountability mechanisms or durability.
- STV/WIGM implementation research is separated into its own reproducible repository because the ranking-cap question is independently reusable and computational.
- Civic/national-service material remains a separate civic-reinforcement hypothesis and is not treated as a substitute for enforceable institutional safeguards.
