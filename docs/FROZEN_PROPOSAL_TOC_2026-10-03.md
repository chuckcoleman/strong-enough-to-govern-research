## Architecture decisions fixed for proposal purposes

- Domestic coercive/military authority is treated as a sixth **provisional** protected function family and receives its own chapter because its legal trigger structure, command chokepoint, timing, and capacity tradeoff are sufficiently distinct from emergency continuation and election administration.
- Courts remain cross-cutting enforcement infrastructure rather than a seventh red line.
- Chapters 11–13 are structural reinforcements, not primary protected function families.
- Chapter 12 remains in the proposal because institutional-redistricting evidence supplies a defensible causal bridge to control of accountability institutions.
- Chapter 13 remains in the proposal but with a narrower claim: Scotland and Portland support STV implementation feasibility, and the completed ranking-cap experiment supplies reproducible ballot-engineering evidence; direct anti-capture or democratic-resilience effects remain hypotheses rather than established findings.
- The Epilogue remains separate civic reinforcement and does not carry the institutional causal argument.
- The final synthesis must preserve both cumulative threat-level resilience and individual dependency sensitivity rather than treating durability as a single score.
